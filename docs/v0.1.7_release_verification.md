## Summary

Version `0.1.7` improves large candidate-pool review with deterministic
selection diagnostics, normalized duplicate grouping, full-pool summaries, and
report-only filtering and ordering. Regulation decisions, emitted candidates,
original candidate indices, and full-pool safety totals remain unchanged by
report views.
